Finally, we conclude that although the base system achieves the lowest average time in the roundabout, it is also the least robust: under certain demand conditions it is more likely to enter a critical congestion state from which it does not recover. In addition, it shows the widest confidence interval, which indicates greater variability and therefore lower predictability.

By contrast, the double synchronised system is the most stable and has the lowest likelihood of collapse, but this stability comes at a cost: it produces the highest average time for vehicles in the system.

The double staggered system provides the best compromise. It improves travel times compared with the synchronised option while still maintaining a high level of robustness. It also yields the narrowest confidence interval, meaning its performance is more consistent and more predictable across replications.
